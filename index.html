1. Examination Rules & Marking Scheme

  • Each question carries 1 mark for a correct response.
  • There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for every incorrect response.
  • Unattempted questions carry 0 marks (no penalty).
  • Personalised teasing/encouragement messages are strictly disabled during the Full Mock to preserve real exam focus.

2. Timer & Auto-Submission Policy

  • The 90-minute countdown clock uses real elapsed-time tracking and auto-saves your answers continuously.
  • Reloading the browser will restore your active session without resetting the timer.
  • When the timer reaches 00:00, the examination will automatically submit.
